# Official sources: data protection laws in the GCC

Official sources only: the legislator's or the regulator's own site. Links were checked on 6 November 2026.

## Qatar (onshore)

- Law No. 13 of 2016 on Personal Data Privacy Protection (PDPPL), Al Meezan, the official legal portal: https://almeezan.qa/legislations/law/2016/13/general
- PDPPL, English translation (PDF), Al Meezan: https://www.almeezan.qa/EnglishLaws//132016.pdf

  - Personal Data Breach Notifications: https://ncsa.gov.qa/shared/document_library/94787172324409/33000/8870/1.0~740b1248-e3a9-40e1-b66e-589091cc182a?mimetype=application%2Fpdf&filename=Personal+Data+Breach+Notifications+-+Guideline+for+Regulated+Entities+%284%29.pdf
  - Special Nature Processing: https://ncsa.gov.qa/shared/document_library/94787172324409/33000/8880/1.0~be43a21b-0e16-448e-8a54-7c58963f421e?mimetype=application%2Fpdf&filename=Guidelines-for-Special-Nature-Processing-EN-v3.pdf
  - Guidelines for Secure Usage and Adoption of Artificial Intelligence: https://ncsa.gov.qa/shared/document_library/94787172324409/33000/9729/1.0~4da3f415-18ce-4925-8ae9-b18d4f26edc9?mimetype=application/pdf&filename=CSSP_Guidelines_for_Secure_Usage_and_Adoption_of_Artificial_intelligence-Eng-v1.0_2%20(2
